It is with profound sadness and loving memories that our family announces the
sudden passing of Jozefa Sobkowicz who, with her loving family by her side, left
us peacefully in her 84th year on Friday, March 1, 2019 in Toronto.

Jozia was born in Kobylowloki, Poland to Jozef and Anna Sobkowicz (predeceased)
and it was in 1970, along with her daughter Bozenka, that she decided to begin a
new life in Canada.

Jozia's strong Catholic faith always remained an integral component of her life.
She was a kind, humble and compassionate woman who always enjoyed spending time
with family and friends. Jozia's selflessness was ever-present until her last
breath. She always put the needs of others before her own and was most joyful
when others were happy. She possessed a generous and deeply loving heart and her
smile lit up the entire room. Jozia was admired, loved and respected by all who
knew her. She enjoyed cooking, gardening, reading, poetry, theater, music,
crossword puzzles and watching hockey games with her grandson. To Jozia the most
important thing in the world was her family. Her last words to her family were
"I love you".

Beloved wife of Leo Richter (predeceased). Dearly cherished and loving Mamusia
and best friend of Bozenka and her husband Ryszard Kuzminski. Loving and proud
Babcia of her precious grandson, Matthew (Mateuszek). Jozia will be deeply
missed, but always lovingly remembered by all her family and friends, both in
Canada and Europe.
